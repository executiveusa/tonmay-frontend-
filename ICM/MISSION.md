# Tonmay — Mission

## Purpose
Tonmay is a photography and select-film portfolio/service site for Seattle and Western Washington.

## Primary audience
People, teams, brands, and organizations evaluating Tonmay for portrait, event, brand, documentary, or select film work.

## Governing message
**Photography with a point of view.**

## Primary action
Start a project / check availability.

## Current product shape
- Public homepage
- Full photography gallery
- Three-step project inquiry flow
- Vercel production deployment

## Design laws
- Photography remains the primary proof.
- Do not invent clients, assignments, testimonials, awards, results, or locations.
- Refine existing architecture before adding sections.
- Mobile is composed independently, not treated as a shrunken desktop.
- Native scrolling and user control remain intact.
- Motion must support hierarchy, orientation, state, or brand character.
- Reduced-motion users must retain comprehension and action.
- The builder does not self-approve a Gauntlet release.

## Current largest experience gap
The portfolio needs more truthful project/assignment context. No client or assignment detail may be added until verified evidence exists.
